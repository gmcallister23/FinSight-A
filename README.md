FinSight is a Financial Insight app that tracks investment portfolios, provides up to date market data and AI feedback on portfolio performance.

FinSight also features an AI chat section to get tips on what to invest in powered by GROQ.

This was a team collaboration project. I was the team lead in this project, I lead project deadlines, assigning tasks, and presentations.

I was responsible for the frontend architecture. I also designed and implement our login JWT system in combination without our backend engineers.

I refined the design of our MarketWatch page.

I fully designed and built the portfolio page which tracks portfolio purchases and sales and feeds the dashboard interface.

To test the site go to: https://fin-sight-a-five.vercel.app/ and register for an account. 

From there add purchase stocks or add existing stocks to your portfolio.  That data will then feed your dashboard and provide AI generated feedback.
